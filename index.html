About ReLoop AI

An Intelligent AI-Powered Resource Discovery, Need-Based Matching and Sustainable Redistribution Platform

🎯 Problem

Reusable items often go unused while others need them. Today, people rely on social posts, informal chats and disconnected donation pages: listings are incomplete, quantities go stale, needs are hard to express, matching is manual and exchanges are not tracked.

💡 Solution

ReLoop AI is one connected platform where owners list resources, seekers describe needs in natural language, and AI agents discover, score and explain matches from live data. Backend rules, not the AI, perform every reservation and quantity change.

📚 RAG pipeline

  1. Approved knowledge documents collected
  2. Text split into chunks with headings
  3. Chunks embedded into vectors
  4. Vectors stored with approval status
  5. User question embedded and compared (cosine similarity)
  6. Top chunks + question sent to Nexus LLM
  7. Grounded answer with source documents; fallback if no match
RAG answers rules. The database answers live availability. They are never mixed up.
